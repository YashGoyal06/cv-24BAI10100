# How to Run VisionGuard

VisionGuard provides a **single, simple command** to launch the project.

---

## Single-Command Execution (Recommended)

Just open your terminal and run:

```bash
python3 main.py
```

### What Happens When You Run `python3 main.py`:
1. **Interactive Visual Window Opens**: A live video window titled **`VisionGuard - AI Driver Safety Monitoring`** appears on your screen.
2. **Real-Time Visual Display & HUD**:
   - Live video stream showing your face with detected facial landmarks.
   - 3D Gaze Ray line projecting out of your nose to show your head orientation.
   - Glassmorphic top Heads-Up Display (HUD) showing the color-coded safety status badge:
     - 🟢 `SAFE`
     - 🟡 `CAUTION` (yawning detected)
     - 🟠 `DROWSY` (prolonged eye closure $\ge 0.5$s)
     - 🟣 `DISTRACTED` (head turned away $>25^\circ$)
     - 🔴 `HIGH RISK` (critical compound fatigue)
   - Real-time Risk Score progress gauge ($0 - 100\%$).
   - Bottom live telemetry bar showing current **FPS**, **EAR** (Eye Aspect Ratio), **MAR** (Mouth Aspect Ratio), **Pose** direction, **Blink counter**, and **Yawn counter**.
3. **To Stop**: Simply press **`q`** or **`ESC`** on the video window (or press `Ctrl+C` in the terminal).
4. **Instant Post-Drive Summary**: The terminal immediately prints your complete driving session analytics and exports timestamped `latest_session.csv` and `latest_session.json` to `data/sample_sessions/`.

---

## Other Run Modes (Optional)

If the evaluator wants to run in headless / automated mode without a GUI window:

```bash
# Pure terminal text streaming mode:
python3 main.py --cli

# Run in terminal mode for exactly 20 seconds:
python3 main.py --cli --duration 20
```
